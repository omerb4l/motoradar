MOTORADAR
DARK · LIQUID GLASS · 1–2 TAPS TO ANYTHING
Three live tabs. Every surface is a frosted panel floating over the content beneath it — nothing pushes a new page. Hit targets are 52–66px for gloved hands, numerals are tabular mono, labels are all-caps at 11px minimum. Tap the map avatars, swipe the ride requests, switch tabs inside any frame.
01 · RADAR — TAP AN AVATAR
02 · COMMS — SWIPE A REQUEST
03 · GARAGE — VERIFIED BIKE
MOTION, ASSIST & RENDEZVOUS
Riding HUD dominates when the wheels turn — speed at 150px, everything else demoted, hold-to-fire SOS within thumb reach. While in motion the app refuses complex input: tap a rider or drag a request and it answers "STOP TO INTERACT". Amber beacons are riders who need fuel, a tube, or a push. Accepted rendezvous pushes a dashed route + ETA onto Radar.
04 · RIDING HUD — HOLD SOS, TAP A PIN
05 · RENDEZVOUS ROUTE + AMBER BEACONS
06 · CHAT — RENDEZVOUS QUICK ACTIONS
ONBOARDING, VERIFICATION & SETTINGS
Signup is two taps to the map. Verification is a single glass drop-zone — tap it to watch the scan run and the badge unlock. Settings leads with Ghost Mode in cold slate-blue (deliberately not the neon green, which always means "live"), then the Spotify link in its own brand green.
